Asset Measurement Practice Quiz (15 Questions) 🧠

1. How is inventory typically measured in financial statements?

A. At its fair value.

B. At the lower of cost and net realizable value.

C. At cost plus accumulated depreciation.

D. At sales price without adjustments.

Correct Answer: B. At the lower of cost and net realizable value.

2. What does net realizable value (NRV) for inventory represent?

A. The original cost of the inventory.

B. The sales price less any costs incurred in the process of sale.

C. The fair value plus selling expenses.

D. The replacement cost of the inventory.

Correct Answer: B. The sales price less any costs incurred in the process of sale.

3. If an inventory item's cost is $100 and its NRV is $80, how should it be valued?

A. $100

B. $80

C. $90 (average)

D. $120 (estimated profit)

Correct Answer: B. $80

4. How are accounts receivable measured in the balance sheet?

A. At their fair value.

B. At the total value less allowance for doubtful debt.

C. At cost minus depreciation.

D. At net realizable value plus interest.

Correct Answer: B. At the total value less allowance for doubtful debt.

5. If total accounts receivable are $50,000 and the allowance for doubtful debt is $5,000, what is the net value?

A. $55,000

B. $50,000

C. $45,000

D. $5,000

Correct Answer: C. $45,000

6. What does the allowance for doubtful debt represent?

A. Expected uncollectible amounts from receivables.

B. Interest income on receivables.

C. Depreciation on receivables.

D. Fair value adjustments.

Correct Answer: A. Expected uncollectible amounts from receivables.

7. What are the initial measurement approaches for Property, Plant, and Equipment (PPE)?

A. Cost or fair value approach.

B. NRV or cost.

C. Fair value only.

D. Depreciation model only.

Correct Answer: A. Cost or fair value approach.

8. Under the cost model for PPE, how is it measured after initial recognition?

A. At fair value.

B. At cost minus accumulated depreciation.

C. At NRV.

D. At cost plus gains.

Correct Answer: B. At cost minus accumulated depreciation.

9. In straight-line depreciation, how is annual depreciation calculated?

A. (Initial value + salvage value) / useful years.

B. Initial value / useful years.

C. (Initial value - salvage value) / useful years.

D. Salvage value / useful years.

Correct Answer: C. (Initial value - salvage value) / useful years.

10. Calculation: A machine costs $10,000, has a salvage value of $2,000, and useful life of 4 years. What is the annual straight-line depreciation?

Formula: (Initial value - salvage value) / useful years

A. $2,000

B. $2,500

C. $3,000

D. $4,000

Correct Answer: A. $2,000
Calculation: ($10,000 - $2,000) / 4 = $8,000 / 4 = $2,000

11. How are marketable securities typically measured?

A. At cost minus depreciation.

B. At their fair value.

C. At NRV less costs to sell.

D. At historical cost only.

Correct Answer: B. At their fair value.

12. If the fair value of marketable securities rises, how is it recorded?

A. As an expense.

B. As income.

C. As a liability.

D. Ignored until sold.

Correct Answer: B. As income.

13. If the fair value of marketable securities falls, how is it recorded?

A. As income.

B. As an expense in the current year.

C. As a gain.

D. Deferred to future periods.

Correct Answer: B. As an expense in the current year.

14. How are intangible assets generally treated if they are internally generated?

A. Recorded at fair value on the balance sheet.

B. Not recorded in the balance sheet.

C. Measured at cost minus depreciation.

D. Valued at NRV.

Correct Answer: B. Not recorded in the balance sheet.

15. Which intangible assets are typically recorded on the balance sheet?

A. Those developed internally.

B. Those acquired from externally.

C. All intangible assets regardless of source.

D. None, as they have no physical form.

Correct Answer: B. Those acquired from externally.
