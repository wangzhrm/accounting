The Accrual Basis vs. The Cash Basis

Case Study: "Creative Cloud Services"

"Creative Cloud Services" is a web design firm that has used a simple cash basis of accounting during its first year of operation. The owner is reviewing their books for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2024. During this review, they have identified the following transactions that require attention:

  • On December 1, 2024, the company paid $1,200 for a one-year insurance policy.
  • On December 15, 2024, they completed a project for a client and sent an invoice for $2,500. The payment is due on January 15, 2025.
  • The company's employees earned $800 in salaries during the last two weeks of December, which will be paid on January 5, 2025.
  • A new client paid $1,500 on December 20, 2024, for a project that will begin and be completed in January 2025.
  • The company purchased a new computer on January 1, 2024, for $3,000. It has an estimated useful life of 5 years with no salvage value.

Prompt: As an accounting consultant, your task is to explain why the cash basis is problematic for "Creative Cloud Services" and to prepare the necessary journal entries to convert their books to the accrual basis.
