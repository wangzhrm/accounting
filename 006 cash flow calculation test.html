CASH FLOWS FROM OPERATING ACTIVITIES

Question 1

Willow Ltd had the following information for the year ending 30 June 2026:

  • Sales revenue: $420,000 (of which $360,000 were credit sales)
  • Opening accounts receivable: $95,000
  • Closing accounts receivable: $110,000
  • No bad debts, discounts, or sales returns

Calculate the cash actually received from customers during the year. Show all workings.

Question 2
