Profit and Cash Position Quiz

This quiz contains 20 scenarios. For each scenario, determine whether the transaction or event affects the company's profit, cash position, both, or neither. After selecting an answer, the correct answer and journal entries will be displayed.

Answer the Following Questions

1. The company receives $10,000 cash from a customer for services performed last month.

2. The company records depreciation expense of $5,000 for the month.

3. The company pays $2,000 in cash for utility bills incurred this month.

4. The company issues $50,000 in new stock for cash.

5. The company performs services worth $15,000 on credit, to be paid next month.

6. The company writes off $1,000 of uncollectible accounts receivable.

7. The company purchases $20,000 of inventory on credit.

8. The company pays $10,000 in cash to settle accounts payable.

9. The company sells equipment for $8,000 cash, with a book value of $6,000.

10. The company accrues $3,000 in interest expense for a loan, not yet paid.

11. The company receives $5,000 in advance for services to be performed next month.

12. The company records a $2,000 expense for prepaid insurance used this month.

13. The company borrows $25,000 from a bank, receiving cash.

14. The company pays $4,000 in dividends to shareholders.

15. The company sells $12,000 of inventory for cash at cost.

16. The company records a $7,000 gain on revaluation of land (under IFRS).

17. The company purchases a 30-day Treasury Bill for $10,000 cash.

18. The company recognizes $6,000 in revenue for services performed this month, paid in cash.

19. The company adjusts an allowance for doubtful accounts by $2,500.

20. The company repays $15,000 of a bank loan principal in cash.
