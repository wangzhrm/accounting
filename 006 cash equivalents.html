Cash Equivalents in Accounting

What Are Cash Equivalents?

Cash equivalents are short-term, highly liquid investments that meet three key criteria in accounting (under IFRS and GAAP standards):

  • Short-term: Original maturity of three months or less.
  • Highly liquid: Readily convertible to known amounts of cash.
  • Low risk: Subject to an insignificant risk of changes in value.

These assets are treated as cash in financial statements because they are stable and easily accessible for immediate use.
