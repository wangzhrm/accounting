Creative Accounting: Income Smoothing at Horizon Corp.

Horizon Corp. manages bad debt expenses to smooth income across two years, with a total bad debt allowance of $1,000,000. In Year 1 (profitable), the manager estimates the bad debt expense. Year 2 expense is $1,000,000 minus Year 1 expense. A Year 1 expense below $500,000 violates accounting standards. Adjust the slider to see the impact on income statements, bonuses, and the stacked bar chart.

Bonus Policy: The manager receives a 5% bonus on net income if net income exceeds $900,000 in each year.

Warning: A bad debt expense below $500,000 violates accounting standards.
