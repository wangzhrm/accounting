Quiz on Differentiating Current and Non-Current Liabilities

This quiz contains 10 questions to test your understanding of current liabilities (due within one year) and non-current liabilities (due after one year). Answer each question by selecting or noting the correct option.

Question 1: Classify the following as current or non-current liability.

Accounts payable to suppliers for goods purchased on credit, due in 30 days.

  • A) Current liability
  • B) Non-current liability

Question 2: Classify the following as current or non-current liability.

Long-term bank loan with principal repayment starting in 3 years.

  • A) Current liability
  • B) Non-current liability

Question 3: Which of the following is typically a current liability?

  • A) Mortgage payable over 15 years
  • B) Salaries payable to employees for the current month
  • C) Bonds payable maturing in 10 years
  • D) Deferred tax liabilities due in 5 years

Question 4: True or False: A portion of a long-term debt that is due within the next 12 months should be classified as a current liability.

  • A) True
  • B) False

Question 5: Classify the following as current or non-current liability.

Unearned revenue from a one-year subscription service, where services will be provided over the next 6 months.

  • A) Current liability
  • B) Non-current liability

Question 6: Which statement best describes non-current liabilities?

  • A) Obligations that must be settled using current assets within one year
  • B) Long-term obligations not due within the operating cycle or one year
  • C) Short-term borrowings from banks
  • D) Accounts receivable from customers

Question 7: Classify the following as current or non-current liability.

Pension obligations payable to retired employees over the next 20 years.

  • A) Current liability
  • B) Non-current liability

Question 8: In a balance sheet, where are current liabilities typically listed?

  • A) Under non-current assets
  • B) Before non-current liabilities in the liabilities section
  • C) After equity
  • D) In the income statement

Question 9: True or False: Accrued interest on a long-term loan that is payable in the next quarter is a non-current liability.

  • A) True
  • B) False

Question 10: Which of the following is NOT a current liability?

  • A) Short-term notes payable
  • B) Dividends payable
  • C) Lease obligations due in 5 years
  • D) Taxes payable

Answer Key (For Instructor Use Only):

  1. A) Current liability
  2. B) Non-current liability
  3. B) Salaries payable to employees for the current month
  4. A) True
  5. A) Current liability
  6. B) Long-term obligations not due within the operating cycle or one year
  7. B) Non-current liability
  8. B) Before non-current liabilities in the liabilities section
  9. B) False (It is a current liability)
  10. C) Lease obligations due in 5 years
