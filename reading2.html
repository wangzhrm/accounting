Global Accounting Standards Overview

A look at the accounting standards used by various countries.

Breakdown of Accounting Standards

A precise percentage breakdown is challenging due to varying adoption levels and local adaptations. However, we can generally classify countries into three main categories based on their primary accounting framework:

  • IFRS Harmonized: Countries that have fully adopted IFRS for most or all public companies. This is the most common category globally.
  • Non-IFRS (Specific National Standards): Countries with their own distinct set of accounting standards, which may or may not be similar to IFRS. The most notable example is US GAAP.
  • IFRS Converged/Adapted: Countries that have developed their own national standards based on IFRS but with some modifications or are in the process of converging with IFRS.
