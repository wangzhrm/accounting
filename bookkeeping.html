Business Story: TechGadgets Inc.

John started TechGadgets Inc. with a $50,000 investment. The business secured a $30,000 bank loan. They purchased $20,000 of equipment. Acquired $15,000 inventory on credit. Paid $5,000 for six months of rent. Made $8,000 in cash sales. Sold $12,000 worth of goods on credit. Purchased additional $10,000 inventory with cash. Paid $7,000 to suppliers. Collected $9,000 from credit customers. Paid $4,000 in employee salaries. Paid $1,200 for utilities. Bought an $18,000 vehicle with $5,000 cash and a loan. Made $15,000 in cash sales during a holiday promotion. Recorded $800 in depreciation. Paid $300 in loan interest. Prepaid $1,800 for insurance. Received a $3,000 customer advance. Wrote off $500 of damaged inventory. Calculated $22,000 cost of goods sold.
