CASH FLOWS FROM OPERATING ACTIVITIES

Question 1

Willow Ltd had the following information for the year ending 30 June 2026:

  • Sales revenue: $420,000 (of which $360,000 were credit sales)
  • Opening accounts receivable: $95,000
  • Closing accounts receivable: $110,000
  • No bad debts, discounts, or sales returns

Calculate the cash actually received from customers during the year. Show all workings.

Credit Sales = $360,000
Opening A/R = $95,000
Closing A/R = $110,000

Cash Received = Credit Sales + Opening A/R − Closing A/R
    = $360,000 + $95,000 − $110,000
    = $345,000

Note: Cash sales ($60,000) are received immediately, so total cash from customers = $345,000 + $60,000 = $405,000. But only credit sales affect A/R.
Final Answer: $345,000 (from credit customers)
Question 2

Oakwood Ltd reported the following for the year ending 30 June 2026:

  • Cost of goods sold: $580,000
  • Opening inventory: $72,000
  • Closing inventory: $65,000
  • Opening accounts payable: $98,000
  • Closing accounts payable: $105,000

Calculate the cash paid to trade creditors during the year. Show all workings.

Step 1: Purchases
Purchases = COGS + Closing Inventory − Opening Inventory
    = $580,000 + $65,000 − $72,000
    = $573,000

Step 2: Cash Paid to Suppliers
Cash Paid = Purchases + Opening A/P − Closing A/P
    = $573,000 + $98,000 − $105,000
    = $566,000

Final Answer: $566,000

Question 5

Birch Ltd had total sales of $680,000, of which $550,000 were on credit. The accounts receivable balance decreased from $142,000 at the beginning of the year to $128,000 at the end. There were no bad debts or discounts allowed.

Calculate the cash received from customers. Show all workings.

Credit Sales = $550,000
Opening A/R = $142,000
Closing A/R = $128,000

Cash from Credit Sales = Credit Sales + Opening A/R − Closing A/R
    = $550,000 + $142,000 − $128,000
    = $564,000

Cash Sales = $680,000 − $550,000 = $130,000

Total Cash Received = $564,000 + $130,000 = $694,000

Final Answer: $694,000

Question 8
