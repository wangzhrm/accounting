Question 1: Which account is affected when a company issues 1,000 new shares for cash?

A) Share Capital

B) Retained Earnings

C) Revaluation Reserve

D) None of the above

Correct Answer: A) Share Capital
Explanation: Issuing new shares for cash is a transaction with shareholders, increasing the Share Capital account.

Question 2: True or False: A net profit increases the Retained Earnings account.

A) True

B) False

Correct Answer: A) True
Explanation: Net profit, when income exceeds expenses, is added to Retained Earnings, reflecting the company's accumulated profits.

Question 3: Which account is affected when a company pays $20,000 in dividends to shareholders?

A) Share Capital

B) Retained Earnings

C) Revaluation Reserve

D) None of the above

Correct Answer: B) Retained Earnings
Explanation: Dividend payments reduce Retained Earnings, as they distribute accumulated profits to shareholders.

Question 4: What causes an increase in the Revaluation Reserve?

A) Issuing new shares

B) Upward revaluation of property, plant, and equipment

C) Payment of dividends

D) Incurring a net loss

Correct Answer: B) Upward revaluation of property, plant, and equipment
Explanation: Revaluation Reserve increases when assets like property, plant, and equipment are revalued upward based on their fair value.

Question 5: True or False: Converting bonds into shares affects the Share Capital account.

A) True

B) False

Correct Answer: A) True
Explanation: Converting bonds into shares is a transaction with owners, increasing Share Capital as debt is exchanged for equity.

Question 6: Which account is decreased when a company repurchases its own shares?

A) Share Capital

B) Retained Earnings

C) Revaluation Reserve

D) None of the above

Correct Answer: A) Share Capital
Explanation: Share buybacks reduce Share Capital, as the company redeems its own shares from shareholders.

Question 7: A company incurs a net loss of $50,000. Which account is affected?

A) Share Capital

B) Retained Earnings

C) Revaluation Reserve

D) None of the above

Correct Answer: B) Retained Earnings
Explanation: A net loss reduces Retained Earnings, as it represents a decrease in accumulated profits.

Question 8: True or False: Share Capital is affected by the company’s profits or losses.

A) True

B) False

Correct Answer: B) False
Explanation: Share Capital is only affected by transactions with shareholders, such as issuing or repurchasing shares, not by profits or losses.

Question 9: Which of the following events increases Share Capital?

A) Earning a net profit

B) Issuing new shares for cash

C) Revaluing assets upward

D) Paying dividends

Correct Answer: B) Issuing new shares for cash
Explanation: Issuing new shares for cash directly increases Share Capital through a transaction with shareholders.

Question 10: Which account is affected when a company revalues its property upward by $30,000?

A) Share Capital

B) Retained Earnings

C) Revaluation Reserve

D) None of the above

Correct Answer: C) Revaluation Reserve
Explanation: An upward revaluation of property increases the Revaluation Reserve, reflecting the increase in asset value.
