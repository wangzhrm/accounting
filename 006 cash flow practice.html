Chapter 11 – Statement of Cash Flows & Cash Controls

Score: 0 / 20

1. What is the primary role of the statement of cash flows?

It reconciles the opening and closing balances of cash (and cash equivalents) shown in the balance sheet.

2. Net cash flows are classified into how many main activities?

Operating, investing and financing activities.

3. Which is not a typical use of the cash-flow statement?

Market share cannot be derived from cash-flow information.

4. Cash equivalents are defined as:

Matches the exact definition given in the slides.

5. Why can profitable firms still fail?

The lecture states “Profitable firms can, and do, fail because of poor cash management…”

6. Under accrual accounting, revenue is recognised when:

Accrual basis recognises income when earned, not necessarily when cash is received.

7. Which transaction affects cash but not profit in the period?

Buying PPE reduces cash; the cost is capitalised, not expensed immediately.

8. Sales $250,000 (all credit), Opening AR $180,000, Closing AR $160,000, no bad debts. Cash received from customers?

Cash = Sales + Opening AR – Closing AR = $250k + $180k – $160k = $270k

9. Formula for purchases of inventory:

Purchases = COGS + Closing Inv – Opening Inv

10. Cash payments to suppliers = Opening AP – Closing AP + ?

Cash paid = Opening AP – Closing AP + Purchases

11. COGS $900k, Open Inv $50k, Close Inv $80k, Open AP $220k, Close AP $230k. Cash paid to suppliers?

Purchases = $900k + $80k – $50k = $930k; Cash = $220k – $230k + $930k = $920k

12. Cash paid for an accrued expense = Opening accrual + Expense – ?

Cash paid = Opening + Expense – Closing

13. Rent expense $120k, Open prepaid rent $15k, Close prepaid rent $5k. Cash paid for rent?

Cash = Closing prepaid + Expense – Opening prepaid = $5k + $120k – $15k = $110k

14. Which activity includes the principal revenue-producing activities?

Operating activities cover the main revenue-producing operations.

15. Negative cash flows from investing activities in a growth phase are:

Growth requires buying PPE, causing negative investing cash flows.

16. Classify: Issue of shares for cash

Equity injection is a financing activity.

17. Classify: Credit sales to customers

No cash changes hands at the moment of the sale.

18. A key internal control for cash is:

Separation of duties reduces the risk of theft.

19. Bank reconciliations should be performed by someone who:

Independence strengthens the control.

20. Which item usually requires adjustment in a bank reconciliation (known to the bank but not yet to the company)?

Bank charges appear on the bank statement first; the company then records them.
