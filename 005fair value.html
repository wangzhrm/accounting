Fair Value Accounting MCQs

1. According to the general principle, how should organizations measure income for goods or services provided?

a) At the cost of the assets received

b) At the fair value of the assets received

c) At the market price of the goods sold

d) At the cash amount paid

b) At the fair value of the assets received

2. What happens if a customer pays for a service with non-cash consideration, such as land?

a) The sale price is based on the cost of the land

b) The sale price is measured at the fair value of the land

c) The sale price is ignored

d) The sale price is based on the organization’s inventory value

b) The sale price is measured at the fair value of the land

3. An organization receives $5,000 cash and land with a fair value of $15,000 for a sale. What is the sales price?

a) $5,000

b) $15,000

c) $20,000

d) $10,000

c) $20,000

4. If land with a cost of $50,000 and a fair value of $70,000 is received instead of cash, what is the sales price?

a) $50,000

b) $70,000

c) $120,000

d) $20,000

b) $70,000

5. An organization sells inventory for $10,000 cash and land with a fair value of $90,000. What is the sales price?

a) $10,000

b) $90,000

c) $100,000

d) $80,000

c) $100,000

6. What is the primary factor in determining the sales price when non-cash consideration is involved?

a) The cost to the buyer

b) The fair value of the assets received

c) The original cost of the inventory

d) The market demand

b) The fair value of the assets received

7. If a customer pays with $8,000 cash and equipment with a fair value of $12,000, what is the sales price?

a) $8,000

b) $12,000

c) $20,000

d) $4,000

c) $20,000

8. Which of the following is NOT a valid method to measure the sales price?

a) Fair value of non-cash assets

b) Cash amount received

c) Cost of the asset to the buyer

d) Total of cash and fair value of non-cash assets

c) Cost of the asset to the buyer

9. An organization receives land with a fair value of $30,000 for a service. What is the sales price?

a) $0

b) $30,000

c) $15,000

d) $60,000

b) $30,000

10. If $15,000 cash and a vehicle with a fair value of $25,000 are received, what is the sales price?

a) $15,000

b) $25,000

c) $40,000

d) $10,000

c) $40,000

11. What should be the basis for measuring income when a customer pays with assets instead of cash?

a) The historical cost of the asset

b) The fair value of the asset received

c) The book value of the inventory sold

d) The estimated future value

b) The fair value of the asset received

12. An organization receives $20,000 cash and a building with a fair value of $50,000. What is the sales price?

a) $20,000

b) $50,000

c) $70,000

d) $30,000

c) $70,000

13. Which of the following best describes the fair value concept?

a) The original cost of an asset

b) The amount an asset could be sold for in an open market

c) The depreciated value of an asset

d) The cost to acquire an asset

b) The amount an asset could be sold for in an open market

14. If a customer pays with $5,000 cash and land with a fair value of $45,000, what is the sales price?

a) $5,000

b) $45,000

c) $50,000

d) $40,000

c) $50,000

15. What is the sales price if only non-cash consideration with a fair value of $80,000 is received?

a) $0

b) $80,000

c) $40,000

d) $160,000

b) $80,000

16. An organization receives $30,000 cash and a machine with a fair value of $20,000. What is the sales price?

a) $30,000

b) $20,000

c) $50,000

d) $10,000

c) $50,000

17. Which factor is irrelevant when determining the sales price with non-cash consideration?

a) Fair value of the asset received

b) Cash amount received

c) Cost of the asset to the customer

d) Total value of consideration

c) Cost of the asset to the customer

18. If $25,000 cash and property with a fair value of $35,000 are received, what is the sales price?

a) $25,000

b) $35,000

c) $60,000

d) $10,000

c) $60,000

19. What is the sales price when a customer pays with $12,000 cash and land with a fair value of $88,000?

a) $12,000

b) $88,000

c) $100,000

d) $76,000

c) $100,000

20. Which of the following reflects the correct approach to measuring sales price with mixed payments?

a) Sum of cash and cost of non-cash assets

b) Sum of cash and fair value of non-cash assets

c) Only the cash amount

d) Only the fair value of non-cash assets

b) Sum of cash and fair value of non-cash assets
