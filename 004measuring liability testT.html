This test paper contains 20 multiple-choice questions on the measurement of liabilities. Liabilities due within 12 months are typically measured at face value, while those due beyond 12 months are measured at present value. Select the best answer for each question by circling the letter.

Question 1: How should a $8,000 utility bill due in 2 months be measured?

A) At face value

B) At present value

C) At fair value

D) At amortized cost

Correct Answer: A) At face value
Explanation: Utility bills due within 12 months are short-term liabilities and are measured at face value, as the time value of money is not significant.

Question 2: A $75,000 mortgage payable due in 15 years should be measured at:

A) Face value

B) Present value

C) Historical cost

D) Market value

Correct Answer: B) Present value
Explanation: Mortgages due beyond 12 months are long-term liabilities and are measured at present value to account for the time value of money.

Question 3: Which measurement method is used for $12,000 interest payable due in 10 months?

A) Face value

B) Present value

C) Fair value

D) Replacement cost

Correct Answer: A) Face value
Explanation: Interest payable due within 12 months is a short-term liability and is measured at face value.

Question 4: True or False: Deferred revenue expected to be recognized in 2 years should be measured at face value.

A) True

B) False

Correct Answer: B) False
Explanation: Deferred revenue due beyond 12 months is measured at present value to reflect the time value of future obligations.

Question 5: A $30,000 supplier invoice due in 1 month should be measured at:

A) Face value

B) Present value

C) Amortized cost

D) Carrying amount

Correct Answer: A) Face value
Explanation: Supplier invoices due within 12 months are short-term liabilities and are measured at face value.

Question 6: Why are current liabilities typically measured at face value?

A) To reflect their historical cost

B) Because the time value of money is not significant for short-term obligations

C) To match their present value

D) To simplify tax calculations

Correct Answer: B) Because the time value of money is not significant for short-term obligations
Explanation: For liabilities due within 12 months, the time value of money is minimal, so face value is used for simplicity.

Question 7: A $90,000 convertible bond due in 4 years should be measured at:

C) Fair value

Correct Answer: B) Present value
