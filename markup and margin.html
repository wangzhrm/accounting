Markup and Margin Practice Test

Instructions

Solve the following problems by calculating the required percentage. Round your answers to 2 decimal places. Write your answers in the blanks provided.

Markup Percentage (MU%): (Selling Price - Cost Price) / Cost Price × 100%

Profit Margin Percentage (MG%): (Selling Price - Cost Price) / Selling Price × 100%

Markup to Margin: MG% = (MU% / (100 + MU%)) × 100
Margin to Markup: MU% = (MG% / (100 - MG%)) × 100

Section 1: Markup to Margin

Calculate the margin percentage for each given markup percentage.

Section 2: Margin to Markup

Calculate the markup percentage for each given margin percentage.
